\section{Abstract}

The project evaluates read-your-writes (RYW), monotonic reads (MR), monotonic writes (MW), and writes-follow-reads (WFR) in a three-member MongoDB replica set. The comparison varies read concern, write concern, and an explicit causal session across the eight C1-C8 cells. Property-specific schedules create stale-secondary and election states. Each operation records logical versions, explicit dependencies, requested and actual routing, response status, fault events, and latency.

The main campaign contains \texttt{\HistoryCount} histories: \texttt{\NormalHistoryCount} normal controls and \texttt{\AdversarialHistoryCount} adversarial trials. The separate \texttt{\PilotHistoryCount}-history pilot is excluded from main estimates. Outcomes remain distinct; \texttt{UNSUPPORTED} histories are not database violations. Claims are limited to the recorded versions, settings, workloads, and schedules.

Among the 960 adversarial histories, RYW had 15 violations in 216 decidable histories (6.9\%). All were in C1 (2/23), C7 (12/14), or C8 (1/30); 16 additional C7 RYW histories were \texttt{INDETERMINATE}. The normal controls also had two MR violations, one each in C5 (1/7) and C8 (1/8); these are not part of the adversarial estimate. No violations appeared among 196 adversarial MR, 160 MW, or 169 WFR histories. One hundred adversarial histories were \texttt{UNSUPPORTED}, all in MW or WFR. These results describe the registered schedules and do not establish universal guarantees.
