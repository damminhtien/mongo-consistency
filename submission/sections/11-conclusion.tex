\section{Conclusion}

Among the main adversarial histories, RYW produced \texttt{\RYWViolationCount} violations in \texttt{\RYWDecidableCount} decidable histories. MR produced \texttt{\MRViolationCount} violations in \texttt{\MRDecidableCount} decidable histories; MW produced \texttt{\MWViolationCount} in \texttt{\MWDecidableCount}; WFR produced \texttt{\WFRViolationCount} in \texttt{\WFRDecidableCount}. These are observations under the registered configurations and schedules, not general guarantees.

The normal controls recorded two MR counterexamples, one each in C5 and C8. In both, the first read observed version 1 and the next read observed version 0 from a different secondary, with no injected fault event. The control results remain separate from the adversarial RQ1 comparison.

The result is limited by \texttt{\AdversarialUnsupportedCount} adversarial trials without the required topology precondition. Pilot histories were excluded, and factorial contrasts describe only cells with decidable histories. This campaign addresses RQ1's configuration comparison; it does not isolate topology as a separate treatment and therefore does not answer RQ2.

All observed RYW violations were in C1, C7, and C8; C7's rate is based on 14 decidable histories because 16 outcomes were indeterminate. No counterexample was observed in the tested guarantee-target cells. These comparisons are finite, schedule-specific observations, and the unweighted factorial contrasts do not support a significance claim.
