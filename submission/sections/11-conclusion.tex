\section{Conclusion}

Of the \AdversarialDecidableCount\ resolved adversarial RQ1 histories,
\AdversarialViolationCount\ violated a tested property and
\AdversarialPassCount\ passed. Under the F3 partition, C1 produced stale-read
and write-order counterexamples. The corresponding C6 histories remained
indeterminate because majority-write timeouts prevented dependent operations
from being issued. All scheduled F2 calls after the election completed, but no
request was made during the election barrier; availability in that interval
was not measured. These findings are specific to MongoDB \MongoDBServerVersion,
PyMongo
\PyMongoVersion, the registered schedules, and the single-host topology. The
RQ4 analysis keeps this boundary explicit: its observed completion and
client-observed all-attempt and resolved p50/p95 values are computed from the
existing histories. Valid timeout timestamps remain in the all-attempt sample,
while PASS/VIOLATION latency has its own denominator; missing partition cells
remain missing evidence.
