\section{Conclusion}

Of the 530 resolved adversarial RQ1 histories, 436 violated a tested property
and 94 passed. Under the F3 partition, C1 produced stale-read and write-order
counterexamples. The corresponding C6 histories remained indeterminate because
majority-write timeouts prevented dependent operations from being issued. All
scheduled F2 calls after the election completed, but no request was made during
the election barrier; availability in that interval was not measured. These
findings are specific to MongoDB \MongoDBServerVersion, PyMongo
\PyMongoVersion, the registered schedules, and the single-host topology.
