\section{Conclusion}

We tested eight MongoDB consistency configurations against read-your-writes
(RYW), monotonic reads (MR), monotonic writes (MW), and writes-follow-reads
(WFR) on a three-member replica set. The configuration campaign covered 256
histories: 96 normal controls and 160 adversarial histories; all controls
passed. C1 returned counterexamples for all four properties. C6 produced no
decidable histories for its four properties: RYW and MR reads timed out, while
MW and WFR histories had unresolved write effects. In the fault campaign, F1
and F2 produced no violations in scheduled calls. F3 produced 56 violations
in four cells: C1 RYW and MW, and C1 and C4 WFR.

The fault campaign covered 432 histories across secondary loss, primary
election, and replication-path partition in a single-host Docker deployment.
These results apply to the tested schedules, timeout settings, and driver
configuration; they do not establish behaviour for other workloads or
deployments. F2 calls began after the election barrier, so the study does not
measure operation completion during an election. Different failure domains
or timeout settings may change whether an operation completes and what state a
client observes.
