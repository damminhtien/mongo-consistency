\texttt{\MainCampaignStatus}. The macros in this section are generated from
\texttt{results/summary/summary.json}. A \texttt{NO\_DATA} state means there is
no measurement to interpret; it must not be read as zero violations.

The consistency denominator contains only PASS and VIOLATION. The adversarial
denominator is \texttt{\AdversarialDecidableCount}; precondition misses,
unavailable operations, unresolved outcomes, and harness errors remain
separate. The pilot is excluded. Each cell is traceable to its configuration,
property, schedule, and raw-history path.

RQ2 is reported separately by fault condition, configuration, and property.
The C1/C3/C4/C6 RQ1 normal controls are its descriptive baseline; normal
histories are not rerun or pooled into the RQ2 campaign. Report each outcome
and operation-availability count with its denominator, and report latency and
rollback measures with their observation counts. Election and recovery
summaries use the completed fault episodes (36 planned: 24 core and 12
partition-extension episodes); the 432 planned histories sharing those
episodes are not independent fault injections. Describe zero observed
violations as a finite observation, not as a zero true violation probability.

The main comparison is configuration by property: prediction, checked
observation, then an explanation supported by the saved trace. Property-specific
denominators and outcome counts are shown with the generated plots.

One C6 RYW history illustrates a protective outcome as unavailability rather
than a stale value. In \texttt{experiment-00018-C6-ryw}, W1 with majority write
concern succeeded on \texttt{mongo3:27017}. While \texttt{mongo1} remained a secondary with
replication isolated, the same causal session issued a majority read there.
The command carried the same \texttt{afterClusterTime} as W1 and timed out after five
seconds with \texttt{NetworkTimeout}; it returned no document. The checker labels the
history \texttt{UNAVAILABLE}, not a stale-read violation. This trace records the
client-visible result and does not expose MongoDB's internal causal counter.

The dedicated MR rerun replaces the original MR slice by \texttt{trial\_id}
in analysis; the earlier raw files remain in the package for audit and are not
counted twice. The rerun yielded 80/80 PASS normal controls and, among the
240 adversarial histories, 120 VIOLATION and 120 UNAVAILABLE; none were
PRECONDITION\_MISS or HARNESS\_ERROR. It contains a direct counterexample in
\texttt{experiment-00286-C7-mr}. C7 used a non-causal session, majority reads,
and a \texttt{w:1} preparation write. Both reads succeeded: the first returned
version 1 from \texttt{mongo3}, then the second returned version 0 from
\texttt{mongo2}. Once the fault was cleared, all three members converged at
version 1. The preconditions were satisfied and the checker classified this
history as \texttt{VIOLATION}.
