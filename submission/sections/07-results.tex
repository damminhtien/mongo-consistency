\subsection{Read-your-writes consistency}

\subsubsection{Prediction}

C6 is the registered RYW target. A completed read older than the preceding
subject write would contradict that prediction.

\subsubsection{Test construction}

The schedule isolates a secondary at version 0, writes version 1 through the
subject session, and verifies that a fresh replica has version 1 while the
isolated member remains stale. The subject then reads from that member. A
completed read of version 0 is the registered counterexample.

\subsubsection{Observation}

Across \RYWHistoryCount\ adversarial histories, the checker recorded
\RYWPassCount\ PASS, \RYWViolationCount\ VIOLATION,
\RYWUnavailableCount\ UNAVAILABLE, and
\RYWIndeterminateCount\ INDETERMINATE. The C6 histories did not produce a
resolved read. The completed stale-read counterexamples occurred in
configurations without an RYW target, as shown in
Table~\ref{tab:rq1-prediction-observation}.

\subsubsection{Interpretation}

The data do not falsify the C6 prediction, but they do not confirm it either:
the required C6 read was unresolved. The completed stale reads are descriptive
evidence from non-target configurations.

\subsection{Monotonic-reads consistency}

\subsubsection{Prediction}

C3 and C6 are the registered MR targets. A completed second read below the
version returned by the first read would contradict the target prediction.

\subsubsection{Test construction}

The schedule confirms that a fresh secondary has version 1 while a stale
secondary remains at version 0. The subject reads the fresh member and then
the stale member in one explicit session.

\subsubsection{Observation}

Across \MRHistoryCount\ adversarial histories, there were
\MRPassCount\ PASS, \MRViolationCount\ VIOLATION,
\MRUnavailableCount\ UNAVAILABLE, and
\MRIndeterminateCount\ INDETERMINATE. There were also
\MRPreconditionMissCount\ PRECONDITION\_MISS and
\MRHarnessErrorCount\ HARNESS\_ERROR outcomes. Neither predicted
configuration produced a completed MR comparison: the required reads were
unavailable or their preconditions were missed.

History \texttt{experiment-00003-C7-mr} is a completed counterexample from a
non-target configuration. Both reads used majority read concern in one session
with causal consistency disabled. The later read returned an older version:

\subsection{Monotonic-writes consistency}

\subsubsection{Prediction}

C4 and C6 are the registered MW targets. A completed converged observation in
which the successor is present without its predecessor would contradict a
target prediction.

\subsubsection{Test construction}

The schedule sends the first subject write to an isolated old primary. After
the majority side elects a new primary, the subject issues a successor write
that names the first write as its parent. An independent observer checks the
same document on all three members after recovery.

\subsubsection{Observation}

Of \MWHistoryCount\ adversarial histories, \MWPassCount\ were PASS,
\MWViolationCount\ were VIOLATION, \MWUnavailableCount\ were UNAVAILABLE,
and \MWIndeterminateCount\ were INDETERMINATE. Both predicted configurations
were indeterminate in every repetition. The completed counterexamples occurred
in other configurations; the recovered document contained a successor without
its predecessor.

\subsubsection{Interpretation}

The campaign provides no resolved MW comparison for C4 or C6. The observed
counterexamples are durable-witness violations in non-target configurations;
they do not establish what happened during the unobserved interval before
convergence.

\subsection{Writes-follow-reads consistency}

\subsubsection{Prediction}

C3 and C6 are the registered WFR targets. A completed converged observation in
which the dependent write is present without the version returned by its
preceding read would contradict a target prediction.

\subsubsection{Test construction}

The schedule creates version 1 on the isolated old branch as setup. The
subject reads that version and issues a dependent write on the newly elected
primary. The write records the read identifier and version. An independent
observer checks the converged document after recovery.

\subsubsection{Observation}

Across \WFRHistoryCount\ adversarial histories, the checker recorded
\WFRPassCount\ PASS, \WFRViolationCount\ VIOLATION,
\WFRUnavailableCount\ UNAVAILABLE, and
\WFRIndeterminateCount\ INDETERMINATE. Every resolved history in predicted
configurations C3 and C6 passed; C6 also had unresolved histories.

\subsubsection{Interpretation}

The resolved C3 and C6 histories did not contradict the registered WFR
prediction. The unresolved C6 histories do not confirm it, and the converged
observer cannot rule out a transient ordering violation before recovery.

\subsection{RQ2 fault-scenario results}

RQ2 completed \RQTwoHistoryCount\ histories in
\RQTwoFaultEpisodeCount\ fault episodes. Its outcomes were
\RQTwoPassCount\ PASS,
\RQTwoViolationCount\ VIOLATION,
\RQTwoUnavailableCount\ UNAVAILABLE, and
\RQTwoIndeterminateCount\ INDETERMINATE. The descriptive violation rate is
\RQTwoViolationCount/(\RQTwoPassCount+\RQTwoViolationCount) =
\RQTwoConsistencyViolationPercent\% among histories with a resolved outcome.

In the F3 signature cells, C1 produced stale-read and write-order violations
in \RQTwoFthreeConeRYWViolationCount/\RQTwoFthreeConeRYWHistoryCount\ RYW and
\RQTwoFthreeConeMWViolationCount/\RQTwoFthreeConeMWHistoryCount\ MW
histories. C6 was indeterminate in
\RQTwoFthreeCsixRYWIndeterminateCount/\RQTwoFthreeCsixRYWHistoryCount\ RYW
and \RQTwoFthreeCsixMWIndeterminateCount/\RQTwoFthreeCsixMWHistoryCount\ MW
histories: its majority write timed out before the read or successor write.
Across \RQTwoMRHistoryCount\ RQ2 MR histories, no violation was observed.

The RQ2 campaign recorded
\RQTwoOperationSuccessfulCount/\RQTwoOperationAttemptedCount\ successful
scheduled operations (\RQTwoOperationSuccessPercent\%) and a history
completion rate of \RQTwoHistoryCompletionPercent\%. Its all-attempt latency
p50/p95/p99 was \RQTwoLatencyMedianMs/\RQTwoLatencyHighMs/\RQTwoLatencyTailMs
ms. The independent observer checked
\RQTwoRollbackCheckedWriteCount\ acknowledged writes and found
\RQTwoRolledBackWriteCount\ rollbacks (\RQTwoRollbackPercent\%).

F2 had \RQTwoFtwoOperationSuccessfulCount/\RQTwoFtwoOperationAttemptedCount\
successful scheduled operations and a median election interval of
\RQTwoFtwoElectionMedianSeconds\ s across \RQTwoFtwoEpisodeCount\ episodes.
The experiment issued no subject request during the election barrier, so this
success rate is not continuous availability. For F3, all-attempt p95 latency
was \RQTwoFthreeLatencyHighMs\ ms across
\RQTwoFthreeOperationAttemptedCount\ timed operations; this includes
\RQTwoFthreeIndeterminateCount\ timed-out operations. Among successful F3
operations, p95 was \RQTwoFthreeSuccessfulLatencyHighMs\ ms, with a different
denominator.
