\section{Results}

The analysis state is \texttt{\AnalysisStatus}; the RQ1 manifest state is
\texttt{\MainCampaignStatus}. The macros in this section are generated from
\texttt{results/summary/summary.json}. A \texttt{NO\_DATA} state means there is
no measurement to interpret; it must not be read as zero violations.

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\caption{RQ1 outcome counts, with normal controls and adversarial histories separated.}
\label{tab:outcomes}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrr@{}}
\toprule
Outcome & Normal controls & Adversarial \\
\midrule
PASS & \NormalPassCount & \AdversarialPassCount \\
VIOLATION & \NormalViolationCount & \AdversarialViolationCount \\
UNAVAILABLE & \NormalUnavailableCount & \AdversarialUnavailableCount \\
INDETERMINATE & \NormalIndeterminateCount & \AdversarialIndeterminateCount \\
PRECONDITION\_MISS & \NormalPreconditionMissCount & \AdversarialPreconditionMissCount \\
HARNESS\_ERROR & \NormalHarnessErrorCount & \AdversarialHarnessErrorCount \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The consistency denominator contains only PASS and VIOLATION. The adversarial
denominator is \texttt{\AdversarialDecidableCount}; precondition misses,
unavailable operations, unresolved outcomes, and harness errors remain
separate. The pilot is excluded. Each cell is traceable to its configuration,
property, schedule, and raw-history path.
