\section{Results}

The analysis state is \texttt{\AnalysisStatus}; \texttt{\HistoryCount} raw histories are available to the report build. The generated table below is populated from \texttt{results/summary/summary.json}. A no-data build uses zero counts and keeps the state label visible rather than copying values from a prediction.

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\caption{Outcome counts in the analysed raw histories.}
\label{tab:outcomes}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lr@{}}
\toprule
Outcome & Count \\
\midrule
PASS & \PassCount \\
VIOLATION & \ViolationCount \\
UNAVAILABLE & \UnavailableCount \\
INDETERMINATE & \IndeterminateCount \\
HARNESS\_ERROR & \HarnessErrorCount \\
UNSUPPORTED & \UnsupportedCount \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The consistency denominator is PASS plus VIOLATION. Operation success, history completion, and the other outcome classes are reported separately. Each decidable group retains a path to its raw histories, configuration ID, property, schedule, and input hashes. Latency uses operation timing; election and recovery intervals use fault-event timing when those events are recorded.
