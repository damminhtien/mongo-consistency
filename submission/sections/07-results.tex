\subsection{Configuration campaign}

\AdversarialHistoryCount\ adversarial histories. The normal controls contain
\NormalPassCount\ PASS and \NormalViolationCount\ VIOLATION outcomes. The
adversarial histories contain \AdversarialPassCount\ PASS,
\AdversarialViolationCount\ VIOLATION, \AdversarialUnavailableCount\
UNAVAILABLE, \AdversarialIndeterminateCount\ INDETERMINATE,
\AdversarialPreconditionMissCount\ PRECONDITION\_MISS, and

\paragraph{Read-your-writes and monotonic reads.}
Across \RYWHistoryCount\ adversarial RYW histories, the checker recorded
\RYWPassCount\ PASS, \RYWViolationCount\ VIOLATION,
\RYWUnavailableCount\ UNAVAILABLE, and \RYWIndeterminateCount\
INDETERMINATE. The corresponding MR counts were \MRPassCount\ PASS,
\MRViolationCount\ VIOLATION, \MRUnavailableCount\ UNAVAILABLE,
\MRIndeterminateCount\ INDETERMINATE, and \MRPreconditionMissCount\
PRECONDITION\_MISS. The target cells did not all produce a resolved comparison.
The completed stale-read trace in a non-target cell is therefore descriptive;
it does not turn an unresolved target into a pass or a failed prediction.

\paragraph{Monotonic writes and writes-follow-reads.}
The MW campaign recorded \MWPassCount\ PASS, \MWViolationCount\ VIOLATION,
\MWUnavailableCount\ UNAVAILABLE, and \MWIndeterminateCount\ INDETERMINATE.
The WFR campaign recorded \WFRPassCount\ PASS, \WFRViolationCount\ VIOLATION,
\WFRUnavailableCount\ UNAVAILABLE, and \WFRIndeterminateCount\
INDETERMINATE. MW and WFR use an independent post-recovery observer, so a PASS
rules out the registered durable counterexample but does not sample every
transient state before recovery.

\subsection{Fault campaign}

The fault campaign completed \RQTwoHistoryCount\ histories across
\RQTwoFaultEpisodeCount\ fault episodes. It recorded \RQTwoPassCount\ PASS,
\RQTwoViolationCount\ VIOLATION, \RQTwoUnavailableCount\ UNAVAILABLE, and
\RQTwoIndeterminateCount\ INDETERMINATE. The resolved violation rate is
\RQTwoConsistencyViolationPercent\% among histories with PASS or VIOLATION.

F1 completed its scheduled operations after a secondary crash. F2 completed
the calls scheduled after the primary election; no subject call was issued
inside the election interval. F3 kept the isolated member reachable to the
client while blocking its replication path. In the signature cells, C1
returned completed stale-read and write-order counterexamples. The matching C6
histories ended in majority-write timeouts before the dependent operation was
issued.
