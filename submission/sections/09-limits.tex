\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}

All three members ran as containers on one physical host. This setup tests
replica behavior under controlled connectivity changes, but it does not model
independent machine failures, wide-area latency, geographic placement, or
independent clock behavior. The Docker VM kernel was recorded; the host
operating-system release was not captured in the campaign manifests.

The workloads are short, synthetic histories chosen to exercise four
client-centric properties. They do not represent arbitrary applications,
multi-document transactions, or every routing and session pattern. Results
apply to the recorded MongoDB and PyMongo versions, image digest, settings,
registered schedules, and operation deadlines. They should not be generalized
to other versions or production deployments without new evidence.

Thirty RQ1 repetitions per adversarial cell and eight RQ2 core repetitions per
cell (twenty in four F3 signature cells) provide finite coverage, not a
probability estimate. Histories grouped under one RQ2 fault episode share a
topology event and are not independent fault injections. F2 deliberately sends
no request during the election barrier, so the experiment cannot quantify
request availability during that interval. C6 timeouts leave some dependent
operations unissued; those cases remain indeterminate rather than being
treated as successful consistency checks.

Outcome explanations use recorded routes, actual server addresses, direct role
observations, session metadata, fault events, and independent post-recovery
snapshots. We did not inspect MongoDB's internal causal state. The conclusions
therefore apply only to the measured client histories and topology.

The MW and WFR checkers use the converged one-document snapshot as a durable
witness. The raw histories do not contain a direct observer sample between the
successor or dependent write and recovery, so a PASS rules out only the
registered durable counterexample. It does not establish that no transient
ordering violation occurred at an earlier instant.

The 23 RQ1 \texttt{PRECONDITION\_MISS} outcomes all occurred in adversarial MR
histories. They are excluded from the consistency denominator and retained as
histories that did not reach the required read state. Among the 240 scheduled
MR histories, 108 were resolved as violations, 109 were unavailable, and 23
missed the precondition; no MR history was classified PASS.
