than the client's preceding operation. Under C6, the dependent causal majority
read timed out; in the matched majority-write history, W1 also timed out. Read
concern determined whether R1 could use the isolated branch's value, while
write concern determined whether W1 could be acknowledged there. The causal
session carried a dependency into the next read.
