\section{Discussion}

The discussion compares each prediction with its checked histories, then identifies the mechanism visible in the trace: member selection, replication lag, election, session ordering, waiting, rejection, or timeout. The requested member is kept separate from the actual server address, so a routing explanation can be checked against the command-monitor record. The factorial contrasts separate read concern, write concern, causal-session, and interaction effects.

A zero violation count means that no counterexample appeared under the tested workload and fault schedule. It does not support a claim about all MongoDB deployments. A high operation success rate also does not imply that returned histories satisfy every client-centric property. \texttt{UNAVAILABLE} and \texttt{INDETERMINATE} are availability or observation limits, not violations.
