\section{Discussion}

\subsection{From stale results to waiting and unresolved operations}

The contrast shows a change in what the client obtains. On a lagging branch,
C1 can return a value that contradicts the client's earlier operation. C6
often withholds the answer or leaves a write unresolved. These observations
are consistent with majority acknowledgement and causal ordering. The
histories record client responses and operation context; MongoDB's internal
wait state is unobserved.

The paired histories explain how the controls changed the observations.
Read concern determined whether R1 could use the isolated branch's value;
write concern determined whether W1 could be acknowledged there; the causal
session carried a dependency into the next read. The completed counterexamples
are visible in the states used by the operations.
